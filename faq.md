# College Student FAQ

## College Timings
The college working hours are from 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM on working days.

## Library
The college library is available for students during the working hours.

## Attendance
Students are expected to maintain the attendance percentage required by the institution.

## Leave
Students should obtain permission from the concerned faculty before taking leave.

## Examinations
Students must follow the examination schedule and instructions issued by the institution.

## Academic Support
Students can contact their respective department faculty or academic coordinator for academic queries.

## Laboratory
Students must follow the laboratory rules and safety instructions while using laboratory facilities.

## Student ID Card
Students should carry their valid student ID card while on the college campus.

## Dress Code
Students should follow the dress code and guidelines prescribed by the institution.

## Library Rules
Students should maintain silence in the library and return borrowed books within the specified period.
